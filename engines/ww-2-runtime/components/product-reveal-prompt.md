## PRODUCT REVEAL (MANDATORY — DO NOT SKIP)

After the mechanism, you MUST formally introduce the product. This is the bridge between "interesting information" and "I need to buy this."

This is NOT just a generic ingredient. This is a SPECIFIC PRODUCT with a brand name, a formula, and a guarantee. You must name all three.

**What to include:**
- Brand name (e.g., "Natural Rems")
- Product name (e.g., "16-in-1 Sea Moss Gummies")
- Formula differentiator (e.g., "sea moss PLUS 15 supporting herbs")
- 2-3 key supporting ingredients with their benefit (e.g., "ashwagandha for stress, turmeric and ginger for inflammation support")
- Form factor (e.g., "Green apple flavored gummy. One per day.")
- Purity claim (e.g., "Natural, purity tested, lab checked")
- Guarantee (e.g., "90-day money-back guarantee")

**How it should feel:** A DISCOVERY, not a sales pitch. "I found these gummies" or "someone told me about these" — not "buy now."

**Source the details from:** `{product_name}`, `{product_price}`, `{product_guarantee}`, `{ingredients_text}` placeholders.
