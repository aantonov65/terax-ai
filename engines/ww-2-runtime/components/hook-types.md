# Hook Types

Hook types define the opening emotional pattern of the ad. Each hook type channels a specific psychological trigger to capture attention and resonate with the reader's pain state.

---

## RAGE: Gender/System Rage

Channels anger at systemic inequality or institutional failure. Expresses fury that the problem exists because "no one cares."

**Pattern:** Outrage + specific inequality + visceral consequence

**Example:** "If men's penises started smelling like rotting fish congress would fund a cure by Friday"

**Usage notes:**
- Most effective for archetypes with strong justice orientation
- The anger must feel JUSTIFIED, not bitter
- Point the rage at systems, not individuals

---

## THIRD: Third Party Alarm

Someone ELSE noticed the problem. The horror of being seen. External validation of internal fear.

**Pattern:** Trusted person + unexpected comment + devastating implication

**Example:** "My hairdresser of 20 years asked if I'd been sick"

**Usage notes:**
- The third party must be someone whose opinion matters
- The comment reveals what the reader has been hiding
- Works well with social-aware archetypes

---

## DESP: Desperate Search

Rock bottom confession about desperate action. The shameful 2am moment. Reveals how far they've fallen.

**Pattern:** Shameful action + specific detail + time marker

**Example:** "I googled 'vagina removal surgery' at 2am"

**Usage notes:**
- Must include specific embarrassing search or action
- Time of day adds authenticity (2am, 3am)
- The search must feel like something they'd never admit

---

## DISMISS: Dismissive Doctor

Channels rage at medical gaslighting. The fury of being told it's "normal" or "in your head."

**Pattern:** Doctor dismissal + specific quote + violent thought

**Example:** "My doctor told me to 'relax more.' I wanted to throw my speculum at her face"

**Usage notes:**
- Quote the exact dismissive phrase
- Include the rage response (even if violent fantasy)
- Validates years of feeling unheard

---

## CONFESS: Dark Confession

Shameful admission they've never told anyone. Breaks the silence around hidden impact.

**Pattern:** Secret behavior + duration/frequency + relationship impact

**Example:** "I haven't let my husband touch me in 8 months"

**Usage notes:**
- The confession must feel like a secret being revealed
- Duration adds weight
- Often involves relationship or intimacy

---

## MISDIAG: Misdiagnosis Reveal

Wrong diagnosis leads to right discovery. The "aha" that explains everything.

**Pattern:** Wrong diagnosis + time wasted + real discovery

**Example:** "I thought I had a yeast infection, but it was actually bacterial dysbiosis"

**Usage notes:**
- Creates an immediate "what if that's me?" response
- The real diagnosis should feel like relief
- Works well when mechanism connects to the misdiagnosis
