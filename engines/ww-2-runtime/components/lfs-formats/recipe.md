FORMAT: RECIPE / FORMULA
Step-by-step protocol. The reader walks away knowing exactly what to do.


LFS V4.1 OUTLINE USE:
This file is the selected native shape for the outline writer. Treat the format as the container, then fill every beat with the source swipe pressure curve, product mechanism, and buyer-facing scene evidence.

The outline must preserve this format's narrator role, emotional engine, beat order, and proof weight. Each beat should name what gets said, what concrete scene or evidence carries it, where the mechanism enters, and where the product earns its first product mention.

Recovery proof, result timelines, victory scenes, and CTA confidence enter after the product or routine discovery has happened. The final script should be planned as feed-native LFS: one thought per paragraph, blank line between thoughts, `========` dividers between sections.

BEAT SEQUENCE:
1. Hook — the result first, then the protocol promise. "Here's the exact protocol I used to [specific result] in [timeframe]." The number or timeframe is the scroll-stop.
2. The problem — compressed. 2-3 sentences max. The recipe reader is already in pain. They need the HOW, not more commiseration.
3. Why nothing else worked — compressed. One paragraph covering the structural limitation of common approaches. Not a full dismantling — just enough to explain why the recipe is different.
4. The discovery — how the narrator found the protocol. Brief source (a professional, a forum, a study). Credibility for what follows.
5. Step 1 — the first action. Specific, concrete. Name what to do, how much, how often. Connect it to the mechanism: why this step matters biologically.
6. Step 2 — the second action. Same specificity. Each step earns the next: "once [step 1] is working, [step 2] can..."
7. Step 3 (optional) — supporting action. Lower weight.
8. The timeline — what to expect and when. Week by week. Specific behavioral markers, not abstract improvement.
9. CTA — practical tone. The recipe reader wants the tool, not the story. Unit math, guarantee, "here's where I got mine."

WEIGHT: 50% on beats 5-8 (the protocol + timeline). The recipe format is defined by actionable specificity. If the steps are vague ("take the right supplements") the format failed. Each step must name what, how much, how often, and why.

WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT: The narrator is a teacher, not a sufferer or investigator. The emotional register is helpful and generous — "let me save you the time I wasted." Pain is the shortest section. The protocol IS the argument. The reader is convinced by the specificity of the instructions, not by emotional weight.
