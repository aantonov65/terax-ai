FORMAT: EXPOSE
Someone uncovering a hidden truth the industry profits from keeping buried.


LFS V4.1 OUTLINE USE:
This file is the selected native shape for the outline writer. Treat the format as the container, then fill every beat with the source swipe pressure curve, product mechanism, and buyer-facing scene evidence.

The outline must preserve this format's narrator role, emotional engine, beat order, and proof weight. Each beat should name what gets said, what concrete scene or evidence carries it, where the mechanism enters, and where the product earns its first product mention.

Recovery proof, result timelines, victory scenes, and CTA confidence enter after the product or routine discovery has happened. The final script should be planned as feed-native LFS: one thought per paragraph, blank line between thoughts, `========` dividers between sections.

BEAT SEQUENCE:
1. Hook — a specific damage number or cost that implies something is wrong with the system, not just the problem
2. Surface-level pain — compressed, 2 moments max. Establish the narrator has lived this.
3. The wrong target — what everyone (including the narrator) was aimed at. Name the conventional wisdom.
4. The hidden truth — the thing nobody talks about. Framed as something the narrator DISCOVERED, not already knew. Specific source: a professional who slipped up, a document, a forum post, a conversation overheard.
5. Why the industry benefits — follow the money. Who profits from the problem persisting? How much do they charge? What do they use themselves?
6. The mechanism — arrives as the smoking gun. The proof that the hidden truth is real. Cause-and-effect chain from the discovery.
7. The search — narrator tries to find a product that matches what they learned. Named dead ends. The product arrives last.
8. The result — compressed transformation. The narrator is now on the other side.
9. CTA — framed as sharing what they found. Peer-to-peer, not selling.

WEIGHT: 40% on beats 4-5 (the revelation). The expose lives or dies on the discovery feeling. If the hidden truth reads like a product feature, the format failed.

WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT: The narrator is an investigator, not a sufferer. Pain is the entry point but curiosity drives the middle. The mechanism is evidence, not explanation. The tone shifts from "I was frustrated" to "I started digging" early — by beat 3.
