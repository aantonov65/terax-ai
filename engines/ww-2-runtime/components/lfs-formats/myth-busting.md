FORMAT: MYTH-BUSTING
Debunking beliefs one by one. Each myth dismantled clears space for the mechanism.


LFS V4.1 OUTLINE USE:
This file is the selected native shape for the outline writer. Treat the format as the container, then fill every beat with the source swipe pressure curve, product mechanism, and buyer-facing scene evidence.

The outline must preserve this format's narrator role, emotional engine, beat order, and proof weight. Each beat should name what gets said, what concrete scene or evidence carries it, where the mechanism enters, and where the product earns its first product mention.

Recovery proof, result timelines, victory scenes, and CTA confidence enter after the product or routine discovery has happened. The final script should be planned as feed-native LFS: one thought per paragraph, blank line between thoughts, `========` dividers between sections.

BEAT SEQUENCE:
1. Hook — "Everything you've been told about [problem] is wrong. Here's what [N] years of [experience/research] taught me." The scroll-stop is the authority to debunk.
2. Myth 1 — the most widely held belief. State it fairly. Then dismantle it with specific mechanical evidence. "This sounds right, but here's why it structurally can't work."
3. Myth 2 — the second most common. Same structure. Each myth builds on the last: "And this one is even worse, because..."
4. Myth 3 — the one promoted by professionals. The reader's doctor/vet/expert told them this. Dismantle respectfully: "They're not wrong about [partial truth]. They're wrong about [the conclusion]."
5. Myth 4 (optional) — the internet myth. The one from forums and social media. Quick dismantle.
6. The truth — what all the myths were hiding. The mechanism arrives as the ONLY explanation that survives the debunking. Every myth pointed at one piece of the problem. The mechanism is the full picture.
7. The product — the implementation of the truth. Arrives naturally from "so what actually addresses this?"
8. The result — compressed transformation. The narrator stopped believing myths and started following the mechanism.
9. CTA — educational tone. "Now you know." The myth-buster has taught the reader something. The product is the application of the lesson.

WEIGHT: 45% on beats 2-5 (the myths). Each myth must be debunked with a SPECIFIC mechanical reason, not just "it doesn't work." The pattern across debunked myths should reveal the mechanism organically — by the time the narrator states the truth, the reader has already figured it out.

WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT: The narrator is a debunker — intellectually rigorous, slightly professorial. The format is structured around what's WRONG, not what's right. The mechanism enters by elimination: everything else has been ruled out, so this must be the answer. The reader feels smarter, not sadder.
