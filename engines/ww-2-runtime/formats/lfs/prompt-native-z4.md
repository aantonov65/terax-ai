STRUCTURE: This is an ESCALATION narrative. The narrator is frightened. Each section builds the case that something is wrong and getting worse. The emotional arc is: alarm → escalating evidence → desperate search → lifeline → relief from fear → cautious stability.

Every paragraph earns the reader's permission to read the next one. Here, permission comes from MOUNTING DREAD — the reader's own fear being articulated, then resolved.

Section 1 — ALARM HOOK (50-80 words) <- immediate, specific
Open with something that just happened. A measurement. A photo. A moment that scared her. NOT backstory. THIS JUST HAPPENED.
- "I measured my stomach last night before bed. 42 inches. I measured it again this morning because I thought I did it wrong. 42 inches."
- "Someone posted a photo of me from last month. I didn't recognize my own body. I made them take it down."
The reader should feel: a jolt of recognition and fear.

Section 2 — THE ESCALATION (250-300 words) <- {a_point} driven — MOUNTING EVIDENCE
Timeline of decline. Specific numbers, dates, sizes. Each paragraph adds proof that this is getting worse. Not stable. Not manageable. ACCELERATING.
"Six months ago I was a size 12. Three months ago, a 14. Last week I couldn't zip a 16."
Include the DENIAL CONFESSION: been telling themselves it's fine. Can't anymore. "I kept saying it was water weight. That it would pass. It's been eleven months."

Section 3 — WHAT I TRIED (150-200 words)
Failed solutions with DESPERATION energy. Didn't try casually — threw everything at it. Money, time, dignity. "I spent $340 on a detox kit. I did a 3-day juice cleanse that made me so dizzy I had to call in sick. I begged my doctor for something — anything."
The failures feel frantic, not resigned.

Section 4 — THE FEAR (150-200 words) <- the thing she's afraid of
The specific fear. Not vague anxiety — a concrete, terrifying possibility can't stop thinking about.
"What if this doesn't stop. What if it keeps getting worse. What if I'm 50 inches by Christmas."
Or health fear: "I started googling what causes sudden abdominal distension. The results scared me so bad I closed my laptop."
This is the emotional peak. Raw, specific fear stated plainly.

Section 5 — THE LIFELINE (300-380 words) <- {b_point} driven — DESPERATE DISCOVERY

Section 6 — THE TURNING (200-250 words) <- {b_point} driven
