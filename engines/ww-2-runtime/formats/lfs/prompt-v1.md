## OUTPUT STRUCTURE (11 Sections Required)

### 1. SHOCK HOOK ← A-POINT DRIVEN
2-3 lines. The scroll-stopper that makes them feel SEEN.

YOUR A-POINT DETERMINES THE HOOK ANGLE:
- A1 (Physical): Open with the visceral body sensation - the smell, the discharge, the physical horror
- A2 (Timing): Open with the age/timing realization - "At 52, I thought menopause was behind me..."
- A3 (Social): Open with the social moment - the husband, the coworker, the public humiliation

The hook MUST match your assigned A-point. If you have A3, do NOT open with a smell description - open with a social situation.

### 2. PERSONAL PAIN SETUP ← A-POINT DRIVEN
2-3 paragraphs. When it started. How it escalated. Continue using your A-point angle.

### 3. GRAPHIC SYMPTOM BREAKDOWN ← A-POINT DRIVEN
Visceral, embarrassing details using A-POINT hotwords. Physical sensations, what they see in the mirror, behaviors to hide it.

### 4. FAILED SOLUTIONS
Everything they tried. Doctor visits with dismissive responses. Supplements that didn't work. Cost totals. End on: "Nothing worked."

### 5. ROCK BOTTOM MOMENT ← A-POINT DRIVEN
1-2 paragraphs. The specific scene where they broke.

If rock bottom type is specified, use it:
- RBPUB: Public humiliation (someone noticed/commented)
- RBREL: Relationship crisis (partner's reaction)
- RBPHOTO: Photo/mirror shock (didn't recognize themselves)
- RBSEARCH: Desperate 2am Google search
- RBEVENT: Specific event ruined

If not specified, choose the most natural fit for the archetype.

Make it cinematic. Specific location, dialogue, feeling.

### 6. DISCOVERY + MECHANISM ← B-POINT DRIVEN
The turn. Two parts:

YOUR B-POINT DETERMINES THE DISCOVERY ANGLE:
- B1 (Mechanism): Focus on the biological "aha" - the science, what's happening in the body
- B2 (Identity): Focus on reclaiming who they are - "I wanted to feel like myself again"
- B3 (System): Focus on the medical system failure - rage at doctors, the truth they were denied

**Part A - The Mechanism Revelation:**
Through late-night research, a forum post, or article - they discover WHY their problem exists. USE THE MECHANISM FROM RESEARCH VERBATIM. Frame the discovery through your B-point lens.

**Part B - The Product Discovery Bridge (CRITICAL):**
They find THE SPECIFIC PRODUCT that delivers this mechanism. NOT "I found some herbs." Write it like this:

### 8. VICTORY STATE ← B-POINT DRIVEN

## FORMATTING RULES (CRITICAL)

THIS OUTPUT GOES DIRECTLY TO META ADS. NO STRUCTURAL MARKERS.

1. NO section headers - don't write "SHOCK HOOK" or "PERSONAL PAIN SETUP" etc.
   WRONG: GRAPHIC SYMPTOM BREAKDOWN
          [content here]
   RIGHT: [content flows naturally]

2. USE section dividers (========) between major sections to create visual breaks

3. Flow naturally within each section using paragraph breaks

4. No bold: Never use **text** or __text__

5. No italics: Never use *text* or _text_

6. Bullet points: Only in symptom breakdown and CTA sections

7. Short paragraphs: 1-3 sentences max

8. Ellipses for trailing thoughts...

9. ALL CAPS for emphasis sparingly (NOT, NOTHING, ZERO)

10. Contractions: Always use (I'm, don't, can't, won't)

The output should read as one continuous personal story, not a structured document.
